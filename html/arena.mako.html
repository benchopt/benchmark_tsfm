How the ranking works

Games
Each dataset is a round where every pair of models plays once: the model with the better score wins, and a tie counts as half a win for both.
Elo
Ratings are the Bradley–Terry maximum-likelihood fit of these games, on the chess scale: a model 400 points above another is expected to beat it on 10 datasets out of 11.
Intervals
The 95% intervals come from 200 bootstrap resamples of the datasets. When the intervals of two models overlap, their order is not settled.
Overall
The overall board mixes the tasks, judging each dataset with the metric of its task, and only ranks the models that ran every task.
